Mashed Potatoe Recipe

Description

A great bowl of mashed potatoes starts with the right potatoes and a few simple techniques. Russets make these mashed potatoes light and fluffy, while warm milk and melted butter help create a smooth, creamy texture without cooling them down. You can add garlic for extra savory flavor, or keep them classic. This easy stovetop side is just as at home with a weeknight dinner as it is on a Thanksgiving or holiday table.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 3 cloves garlic, peeled, or to taste (Optional)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all Ingredients
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and garlic, if using:
    reduce the heat to medium and simmer until potatoes are easily pierced with a
    fork, about 15 minutes.
  3. When the potatoes are almost finished, heat milk and butter in a small saucepan
    over low heat until butter is melted.
  4. Drain potatoes and garlic well; return to the hot pot for 1 to 2 minutes to remove
    excess moisture. Gradually add the warm milk mixture while mashing until
    smooth and creamy, using only as much as needed to reach your desired
    consistency.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve topped with extra butter, if desired.
