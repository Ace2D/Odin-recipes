Cranberry Sauce Recipe

Description

Skip the store-bought stuff this year and serve homemade cranberry sauce.
It's actually way easier than you think—you can make this top-rated recipe, which
has more than 3,000 rave reviews from Allrecipes community members, with just three ingredients to deliver fresh, vibrant flavor you can't get from a can.
It thickens as it cools, making it an easy side to prepare ahead for Thanksgiving,
Christmas, or any festive meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup orange juice
  • 2 ounces fresh cranberries

Steps

  1. Combine sugar and orange juice in a medium saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until the sugar dissolves and the mixture begins to simmer.
  2. Add cranberries and cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until most of the berries pop and the sauce begins to thicken, 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and transfer sauce to a bowl. Let cool; the sauce will continue to thicken as it cools. Serve at room temperature or cover and refrigerate until chilled.
